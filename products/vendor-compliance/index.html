Built for whichever side of the invoice you're on

Most companies lose money in both directions: customers deduct more than they should, and suppliers get paid more than they should. Pick the side that costs you more and start there.

Deduction and chargeback recovery

Questions

Supplier recovery FAQ

What is supplier recovery?

Finding and reclaiming money lost in transactions with trading partners. For a supplier or distributor, that usually means disputing invalid customer deductions and chargebacks. For a buyer, it means recovering duplicate payments, overpayments and vendor credits that were never applied.

Do I need to integrate with my ERP?

No. It works from a CSV or Excel export: a deduction log if you're the supplier, or an AP payment history if you're the buyer. Any ERP or accounting system that can export to a spreadsheet works.

Is my data uploaded when I try the demo?

No. The demo reads your file inside your browser. The file is never sent to our servers.

Does anything get sent to my customers or vendors automatically?

No. Every claim is drafted for review. Nothing goes out until someone on your team approves it and sends it.

Which deductions and payments does it flag?

Shortages against signed PODs, on-time fines on on-time deliveries, unauthorized promo and co-op deductions, pricing claims at the agreed price, compliance fines, damage and return deductions, and duplicates. On the payables side: duplicate and near-duplicate payments, payments above invoice, invoices above PO, unapplied credit memos and missed early-pay discounts.

What is the free recovery review?

You send us an export. We run it, check the strongest findings against your documentation, and send back a one-page report with verified dollars, likely dollars and the claims we'd file first.
